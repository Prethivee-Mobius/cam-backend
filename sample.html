LEASE AGREEMENT — ARTICLE 7

7.1  Common Area Maintenance Charges. Tenant shall pay its Proportionate Share of Common Area Maintenance ("CAM") expenses incurred by Landlord in the operation, management, and maintenance of the Common Areas of the Shopping Center.

7.2  Annual Cap on Controllable Expenses. Notwithstanding the foregoing, increases in Controllable Operating Expenses in any Lease Year shall not exceed five percent (5%) of the Controllable Operating Expenses for the immediately preceding Lease Year, calculated on a cumulative, compounding basis.

7.3  Exclusions from CAM. CAM expenses shall expressly exclude: (a) capital expenditures except as amortized in accordance with Section 7.5; (b) depreciation; (c) leasing commissions; (d) costs of correcting defects in original construction; and (e) management fees in excess of four percent (4%) of gross rents collected from the Shopping Center.

7.4  Reconciliation. Within one hundred twenty (120) days following the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall furnish Tenant a statement of actual CAM expenses for such year, together with Tenant's Proportionate Share thereof.
